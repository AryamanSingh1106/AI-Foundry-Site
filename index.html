Vision

To establish AI Foundry as a dynamic innovation hub that transforms real-world problems into impactful AI-driven solutions by fostering creativity, rapid prototyping, entrepreneurship, and technology-led innovation for society and the university.

Why we exist

Ideas are everywhere. Finished solutions are rare. Membership here is measured in work, not attendance — every member belongs to a team, and every team is building something.
